GREENSBORO HAIRCARE — what to upload
====================================

WHAT WENT WRONG
Your site has the catalogs folder from an earlier download, which contains
5 PDFs. The Guest Consultation Form was added later, so its PDF
(consultation-form.pdf) was never in that folder — the app looked for it,
didn't find it, and that's the error you saw. My fault, not yours.

THE EASY FIX — upload index.html only
-------------------------------------
Replace index.html in your repo with the one in this folder. Done.
All six sheets, consultation form included, are built INTO that file. They
open and print with no folder at all. You can even delete the catalogs
folder from your repo and everything keeps working.

IF YOU WANT PDF DOWNLOAD BUTTONS TOO (optional)
-----------------------------------------------
1. Upload index.html AND the whole catalogs folder from here (6 PDFs now —
   consultation-form.pdf is the one that was missing).
2. On your site, tap the gear, password Artis.
3. Store & tax tab -> tick "I also uploaded the /catalogs folder".
4. Save.

Now each sheet also shows "Download the PDF" for the full-resolution file.
Leave that box unticked and nothing breaks — the built-in viewer still works.

AFTER UPLOADING
Hard-refresh: Ctrl+Shift+R on Windows, Cmd+Shift+R on Mac.
